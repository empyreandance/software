Herelore Privacy Policy

Cooke Studios LLC · Effective August 10, 2026

Herelore answers one question — what happened here? — from public records. It is built to know as little about you as possible while doing that. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

What we collect, and why

  • Your location, only when you ask. When you tap a spot, start a tour, or turn on Road-Trip Radio, your device sends coordinates to our server so we can gather the public record for that place and write its story. During an active tour or radio session, your position is used continuously so narration can follow the road. We do not track you in the background, we do not build a history of where you go, and location is never used for advertising.
  • A random install identifier. A random code, created on first launch and tied to your device installation rather than your identity, accompanies requests so we can enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse.
  • Requested coordinates in server logs. Our server keeps a log of coordinates that were asked about (with the random identifier) for rate-limiting, abuse prevention, and cost control.
  • Email and name, only if you sign in. Signing in with Apple (optional; used only to sync saved places) shares the name and email you approve — including Apple's private relay address if you choose Hide My Email.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We receive Apple's signed transaction records to activate premium features; we never see your payment details.
  • Things you send us. Story reports ("See something wrong?") and address-removal requests include whatever you type, and reports include the location of the story being reported.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • We never sell or rent your information — to anyone, for anything.
  • We do not build profiles of you or attempt to identify anonymous users.

Who processes data on our behalf

Supabase (our database and server host), Anthropic (coordinates and public- record excerpts are sent to generate narratives — never your name, email, or identifier), Apple (sign-in and purchases), and Resend (delivers our internal alert emails). Each receives only what its role requires. The historical content itself comes from public sources such as the Library of Congress, the National Park Service, NOAA, the U.S. Census Bureau, Wikimedia projects, and the Digital Public Library of America.

On-device by design

The neural narrator generates speech entirely on your phone — no audio or text-to-speech requests ever leave your device. Your reading passport, story cache, and preferences also live on-device.

Retention and deletion

  • Saved places persist until you delete them or your account.
  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and saved places from our servers.
  • Generated stories are cached server-side (typically up to 180 days) keyed to locations, not to people. Request logs are retained for abuse prevention and periodically pruned.
  • Content on your device is removed by deleting the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission is yours to grant or revoke in iOS Settings; the app remains usable with featured places without it.
  • Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may request access to or deletion of their data; in-app account deletion honors this immediately, and anything else can be requested by email.
  • If a story concerns your own address and you want it excluded, use Settings → "Remove information about my address" — a human reads every request.

Children

Herelore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update it here with a new effective date and note it in the app's release notes.
